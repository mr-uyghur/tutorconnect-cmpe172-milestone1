# TutorConnect Policies and FAQ

This is the knowledge source for the TutorConnect assistant. Each "##" section is stored as one document in the vector store.

## Hours of operation
Tutoring sessions run Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM Pacific Time. There are no sessions on Sundays or on SJSU campus holidays. The support desk answers messages Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## Session format and length
Every session is exactly one hour and starts on the hour (for example 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM). Sessions are held online; the video link is included in the booking confirmation message. Students should join on time; the tutor waits 15 minutes before marking the student as a no-show.

## Services offered
TutorConnect offers two services. Java Fundamentals covers syntax, object-oriented design, collections, exceptions, and debugging, taught by Alex Chen. SQL and Databases covers relational design, joins, indexes, transactions, and query tuning, taught by Sam Rivera. Sessions are free for enrolled SJSU students.

## How to book a session
Sign in with a student account, browse open slots, and book one. A slot can hold only one booking: if two students try to book the same slot at the same time, only one booking succeeds and the other student is asked to pick a different slot. You can only book slots that have not started yet. Tutors cannot book sessions for students.

## Cancellation policy
Students can cancel their own booked session at any time before it starts, from the My appointments page or through the assistant. Cancellation is free. We ask for at least 12 hours notice as a courtesy so another student can take the slot. A session that has already started cannot be cancelled. When a session is cancelled, the slot becomes open again for other students.

## Rescheduling
There is no separate reschedule action. To move a session, book the new time first and then cancel the old one, so you do not lose your spot if the new time is taken.

## No-show policy
If a student does not join within 15 minutes of the start time, the session is marked as a no-show. After three no-shows in one semester, the student can only book sessions up to 48 hours in advance for the rest of that semester.

## Reminders and notifications
Students receive a confirmation message when they book and a cancellation message when they cancel. About 24 hours before a session, TutorConnect sends one personalized reminder with the session details and a short preparation tip. Messages go to the email address on the student's account.

## How to prepare for a session
Bring a specific question or the code you are stuck on. For Java sessions, have your project open in an IDE that compiles. For SQL sessions, have the schema and the query you are working on ready. Sharing the assignment prompt with the tutor at the start saves time.

## Accounts and privacy
Each student can only see and cancel their own appointments. Tutors can see the sessions booked with them. The assistant acts as the signed-in user and cannot see or change another student's bookings. Passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes and are never shown to staff.

## Using the AI assistant
The assistant can find open slots, list your appointments, book a slot, and cancel a booking. It always shows a summary and waits for you to confirm before it books or cancels anything. Policy questions are answered from this document; if the answer is not here, the assistant says it does not know and suggests contacting the support desk.

## Contact and support
For problems the assistant cannot solve, email support@tutorconnect.example.test. Include your account email and the date and time of the session.
